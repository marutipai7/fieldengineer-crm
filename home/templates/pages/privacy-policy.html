1. Introduction

Field Engineer ("FE", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Field Engineer platform, including our website, mobile applications and related services (collectively, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you use our Services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us and information that is collected automatically when you use our Services.

  • Personal Information: name, email address, phone number, company details, billing information, government ID (for verification).
  • Usage Information: booking details, device information, IP address, pages visited, and app interactions.
  • Location Information: real-time location of engineers (only during active jobs) to provide live tracking and job updates.
  • Documents & Files: reports, images, invoices and other files uploaded to the platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain and improve our Services, including:

  • Manage bookings, track jobs and communicate updates.
  • Verify identities, prevent fraud and ensure platform security.
  • Process payments, invoices and other financial transactions.
  • Generate reports, analytics and operational insights.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following cases:

  • With vendors and engineers: to assign jobs and deliver services.
  • With service providers: who help us operate our platform (hosting, analytics, payments, communication).
  • For legal reasons: when required by law or to protect our rights, safety or property.
  • With your consent: or at your direction.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Our platform uses encryption, secure servers and strict access controls.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to access, update or delete your personal information. You can also opt-out of marketing communications or request data portability where applicable.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer.

About the Role

At FE – FieldEngineer, we're building India's most trusted B2B field service marketplace. As a Senior Software Engineer, you'll design and build scalable backend services, improve platform performance, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable solutions for thousands of businesses and engineers.

You'll work closely with Product, Design, QA, and Engineering teams to build features that directly improve the experience of customers and field engineers across India.

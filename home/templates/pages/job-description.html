About the Role

At FE – FieldEngineer, we're building India's most trusted B2B field service marketplace, connecting thousands of businesses with verified, skilled engineers across the country.

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will be at the heart of this mission—designing and building the systems that power job matching, real-time tracking, secure payments, and mobile experiences for both customers and engineers.

You'll work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment alongside talented engineers, product managers, and designers.

This is a high-impact role where your code directly shapes how businesses find reliable engineers and how engineers grow their careers on our platform.

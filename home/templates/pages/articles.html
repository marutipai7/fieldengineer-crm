How to Post Your First Job on FE

{# Author Widget #}
FE

FE Support Team

Help Center

{# Intro Paragraph #}

Posting your first job on FE – FieldEngineer is simple and takes less than 5 minutes. Once your request is live, verified field engineers in your area will review the details and submit their applications. This guide walks you through every step so you can get started with confidence.

{# Section: What You'll Need #}

What You'll Need

  • An active FE account (sign up free at fieldengineer.com)
  • Your site address and contact person details
  • A clear description of the work required and an approximate budget
{# Section: Step-by-Step #}

Step-by-Step: Posting Your Job

{# Step 1 #}
1

Log in and go to your Dashboard

Once logged in, navigate to your customer dashboard. You will see a prominent 'Post a Job' or 'Create New Request' button in the top action bar. Click it to begin the job creation flow.

{# Step 2 #}
2

Select a Service Category

Choose the service category that best describes the work needed — for example, Electrical, Networking, HVAC, or Facility Management. Sub-categories help engineers understand the scope instantly and improve match quality.

{# Step 3 #}
3

Describe the Work & Set Your Budget

Write a clear, detailed description of what needs to be done. Include expected hours, any special tools or certifications required, and site access details. Set a realistic budget range using the slider — competitive budgets attract more qualified engineers. Note: FE Admin does NOT assign engineers — you always review all applicants and personally choose the right fit for your job.

{# Step 4 #}
4

Add Site Details & Upload Documents

Enter the full site address, contact person name, and any parking or access instructions. Upload supporting documents such as floor plans, site photos, network diagrams, or previous job reports. Well-documented jobs receive higher quality applications.

{# Step 5 #}
5

Review and Submit

Review all details on the summary screen. Once satisfied, click Submit Job Request. Your request will be instantly visible to verified engineers nearby. You will receive a confirmation email and can track applications in real time from your dashboard.

{# Section: Tips for a Better Job Post #}

Tips for a Better Job Post

{# Card 1 #}
check_small

Be Specific About Scope

Vague job descriptions attract fewer applicants. Specify the exact issue, the environment (office, warehouse, data center), and any compliance requirements.

{# Card 2 #}
check_small

Upload Clear Photos

A picture is worth a thousand words. Photos of the equipment, fault, or site layout help engineers submit accurate bids and arrive better prepared.

{# Card 3 #}
check_small

Set a Realistic Budget

Research typical rates for your category. Jobs with realistic budgets fill 3x faster than those with below-market ranges. You can always negotiate once engineers apply.

{# Section: What Happens After You Post? #}

What Happens After You Post?

Once your job is live, here is what to expect:

  • arrow_forward Nearby verified engineers receive an instant notification about your request
  • arrow_forward Engineers review your job details and submit their applications with profiles and bids
  • arrow_forward You compare applicants, check ratings and certifications, and award the job to your chosen engineer

The whole process typically takes just a few hours from posting to awarding. Ready? Post your first job arrow_forward
